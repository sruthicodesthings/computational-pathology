# Scientific scope and interpretation boundaries

The internship deliverable was a computational pipeline and measurements for ongoing biological investigation, **not** a completed longitudinal biological conclusion.

- AP-based trapezoidal integration estimates volume from areas of sampled sections; it does not track the same inclusion growing within a living mouse.
- Different experimental timepoints represent different sampled animals unless explicitly established otherwise.
- Registration and segmentation errors propagate into region-level measurements.
- Nearest-neighbor MERFISH matching indicates spatial association with reference cells, not proven cell-of-origin or causation.
- The biological team, not the computational intern, was responsible for interpreting disease mechanisms.
- The project report's broad biological conclusions should not be reproduced as independently established findings in recruiter-facing materials.
